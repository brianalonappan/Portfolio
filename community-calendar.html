Community Calendar

I manage a public community calendar that lists dance events from across the city, sorted into four categories: Festivals, Performances, Intensives & Workshops, and Convenings.

Getting an event into the right category is what makes the calendar actually useful, if everything gets dumped into one bucket, nobody can find what they're looking for. (The organizations and events below are invented for this demo, not real submissions.)

Real submissions aren't nearly this clean, plenty of events could plausibly fit two categories, and the deadline pressure is real every single week.

Consistent categorization is what lets a member actually find what they're looking for. Someone scanning for a weekend workshop shouldn't have to wade through every festival and performance in the city to find it, the category someone chooses determines whether their event gets seen at all.
