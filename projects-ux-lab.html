Usability Lab

In User-Centered UI/UX Design and in this project's own playtests, I sort what testers tell me by how much it hurts. Severe means the core flow breaks, or every tester hit it. Moderate means it slows people down. Low means polish.

Each round gives you six tester notes. Sort each one by severity. (Every note below is invented for this demo.)

The rule I work by: fix what breaks the core flow first, then what slows people down, and treat polish as the last pass.

Sorting before fixing keeps a loud small request, like a color change, from jumping ahead of a quiet problem that stops people from finishing.
