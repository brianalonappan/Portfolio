Build It or Cut It

On Chop, Cook, Serve! our team of three had six weeks. The rule that saved the project: build one cooking mechanic really well, and cut anything that needs a brand-new one.

Each round gives you six feature ideas for a small cooking game. Decide which to build and which to cut. (These ideas are for practice. They are not a list of what shipped.)

The rule I work by: when time and team size are fixed, reuse what already works. A new variant of the same mechanic is cheap. A new mechanic is a whole new project.

Depth over breadth: one well-built mechanic beats three rushed ones, especially under a hard team-size and time constraint.
