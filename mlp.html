Member Landing Page

I maintain a members-only page that lists opportunities such as grants, auditions, and open calls. Two rules keep it useful: open items are listed in order of deadline, soonest first, and anything that has closed moves to the bottom with a standard note instead of being deleted.

Rebuild the page by clicking the opportunities in the order they belong. Three wrong picks and the round ends. (Every opportunity below is invented for this demo.)

Closed opportunities stay on the page, moved to the bottom with a short note, instead of being deleted. Members sometimes come back to check a deadline they remember, or to find who to contact next time it opens.

The page's job is to surface what's still actionable first without destroying the record of what came before. Sorting by deadline does the first part, and the demotion with a note does the second.
