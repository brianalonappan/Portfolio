Projects & Coursework — Chop, Cook, Serve!

For Interactive Entertainment Engineering, my 3-person team built a cooking and time-management game in under six weeks, inspired by Cooking Mama, Papa's Pizzeria, and Stardew Valley.

Early on, we had to make a scope call about how many food items the game would actually support.

What would you do?

You're a team of 3 with 6 weeks. You originally planned pizza, salad, and beverages, each with its own cooking mechanic. What's the scope call?

Here's what we actually did:

We originally planned multiple food items: pizza, salad, and beverages. But each one needed its own separate cooking mechanic (chopping, mixing, timing logic), and building three solid mechanics wasn't realistic for a 3-person team in 6 weeks.

So we scoped down to two pizza variants, pepperoni and cheese. That let us build one cooking mechanic really well and still give players meaningful choice and variety, instead of three shallow, half-working ones.

Depth over breadth: one well-built mechanic beats three rushed ones, especially under a hard team-size and time constraint.
