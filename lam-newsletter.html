LA Moves Newsletter

I produce LA Moves, a weekly newsletter sent to DRC members every Monday or Tuesday.

Each section has a strict cap, to keep the newsletter scannable. Some weeks, more relevant events and opportunities come in than fit, including some with dates still months away.

Here's what I actually did:

I keep a running hold list of events and opportunities that are worth sharing but too far out to earn one of the limited slots in a given week, things like a ballet festival three months away, or a submission deadline still weeks off.

Each week, I check that list against the calendar and bring items back in once they fall within the current month, or the next month if we're nearing the end of the current one. The items with the nearest dates and deadlines get the limited slots now; the rest wait their turn. That way nothing valuable gets permanently dropped just because it showed up too early, and the newsletter itself never gets bloated or hard to scan.

The cap isn't really about limiting good content, it's about protecting the reader's attention. Sorting by urgency every week does more for the member reading the newsletter than cramming everything in the moment it arrives.
