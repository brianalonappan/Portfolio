The Weekly Newsletter

I help put together a weekly newsletter for a dance community. Every section has a hard cap on items, so the issue stays easy to scan.

More good items arrive than fit. The skill is deciding what goes in this week, and what waits on a hold list until it's closer to its date. (Every event and organization below is invented for this demo.)

Each round, six new submissions show up and there are only 3 slots. You have 45 seconds.

The rule I work by: the items with the nearest dates and deadlines get this week's slots, and everything else waits on a hold list until it's close enough to matter.

The cap isn't about limiting good content. It protects the reader's attention. Holding an item for the right week serves members better than cramming everything in the moment it arrives, and nothing valuable gets dropped just because it showed up early.
