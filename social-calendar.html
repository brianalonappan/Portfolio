Social Media Calendar

I plan social posts for a dance organization, with two goals: grow an authentic following and bring more people onto the email list.

A post only works if it lands at the right moment. Too early and people forget, too late and they've missed it. (Every event below is invented for this demo.)

Each round gives you five post ideas and a Monday to Friday week. Put each post on the day it should go out. You have 90 seconds, or you can turn the timer off.

Week scheduled

Real calendars have more moving parts: several platforms, cultural observances, and events that shift. But the core question for every post is the same: when will the right person actually act on it?

Timing is part of the message. A reminder that arrives two days before an event gets people to register, while the same reminder a week early gets scrolled past. Planning the whole week at once also keeps posts from piling up on one day.
